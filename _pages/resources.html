--- layout: archive title: "Leadership, Community & Academic Resources" permalink: /resources/ author_profile: true --- A curated repository of community initiatives, peer-led postdoctoral advocacy, and evidence-based resources advancing equity and talent retention across STEM disciplines. --- ## Postdociot: The International Postdoc Network Founded during early 2020, **Postdociot** is an independent international community initiative created to connect, support, and mentor postdoctoral researchers across structural biology, chemistry, and life sciences. The organization provides peer support frameworks, scientific seminar series, and career advancement panels across international cohorts. ### Community Channels & Connections * **Community Group:** [Postdociot Discussion Network](https://www.facebook.com/groups/916327312206421/) * **Community Updates:** Connect via [@Postdociot](https://x.com/postdociot) * **Member Features & News:** [Submit milestones and research achievements](https://forms.gle/6qwgqAwJfp2LHZ697) * **Genesis & Mission:** [Read the founding background](https://mirikrupkin.github.io/posts/2020/07/blog-post-1/) ### Selected Seminars, Panels & Community Roundtables * **Career Development Roundtable:** Navigating cross-institutional transitions and faculty job searches. * **Finding an International Postdoc:** Demystifying visa logistics, laboratory selection architectures, and fellowship funding. * **Peer Mentorship Coffee Series:** Regular interactive forums connecting early-stage postdocs with senior academic fellows.
