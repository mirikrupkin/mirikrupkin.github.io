Computational Software Suites & Open-Source Platforms
======
* **[FoldCheck-RNA ↗](https://github.com/mirikrupkin/foldcheck-rna):** Automated nucleic acid structural validation suite evaluating generative predictions (AlphaFold 3) against empirical Cryo-EM and crystallographic ground truth. Implements coarse-grained phosphorus (P) backbone superimposition and an extensible 30+ non-canonical nucleotide modification registry (`Python, BioPython, SciPy, PyTest`).
* **[FoldCheck-Pro ↗](https://github.com/mirikrupkin/foldcheck-pro):** High-throughput validation pipeline for protein assemblies leveraging the Hungarian matching algorithm (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`) to resolve asymmetric heterodimer subunit pairing (HIV-1 RTase p66/p51) and automated dynamic pLDDT/RASA surface classification (`Python, NumPy, SciPy, REST APIs`).

* **Automated Pipeline & Generative AI Architecture:** Architected automated Python and MATLAB data pipelines integrating custom image analysis and kinetic fitting routines, eliminating manual bottlenecks across multi-gigabyte Cryo-EM and single-molecule datasets.
* **Consortium Milestone Leadership (NIH CHEETAH):** Serve as technical milestone lead and primary scientific speaker within the Capsid & Core Architecture subgroups of the NIH-funded CHEETAH consortium; supervised 3 graduate researchers across structural workflows (yielding 3 publications).
